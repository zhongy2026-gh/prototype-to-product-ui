## Business usability

- [ ] The operator can identify the next required action without technical knowledge.
- [ ] Current progress, pending decisions, previews, and final results are prominent.
- [ ] Advanced controls remain accessible without dominating the primary workflow.
- [ ] Destructive, expensive, or externally visible actions retain confirmation boundaries.

## Visual fidelity

- [ ] Shared shell, content width, grids, spacing, and visual hierarchy match the approved direction.
- [ ] Each required route was compared with its corresponding reference.
